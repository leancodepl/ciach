The design system's fonts, as its export ships them and as leancode.co serves
them: Cabinet Grotesk at weights 500, 600 and 700 for display and headings,
Switzer at weights 400 and 600 for body and UI. Both are Fontshare fonts by
the Indian Type Foundry under the ITF Free Font License
(https://www.fontshare.com/licenses/itf-ffl). Each file is one weight of the
Latin and Latin Extended set, 335 to 340 glyphs, without the IPA block or
arrows, which fall back to the next family in the stack.
